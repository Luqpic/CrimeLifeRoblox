Upload one reference image from this folder, then paste the shared block followed by the matching view block.

## Shared block (paste first, every time)

Use the attached image as a strict layout and massing reference. Keep every building, wall, tower, road and open space in exactly the same position, size, shape and proportion, and keep the same camera angle and framing. Do not add, remove or move any structure. Only design the materials, surfaces and details on top of it.

Style: a polished semi-low-poly Roblox tycoon game environment. Chunky simplified shapes, clean flat-shaded surfaces, crisp edges, a few strong details per surface, soft global illumination, gentle ambient occlusion. Bright late-afternoon daylight with soft shadows.

Theme: a criminal gang hideout disguised as a tired scrap-and-logistics yard. Weathered grey concrete walls, corrugated metal panels in rust orange and muted teal, dark-stained wooden decking, black steel window frames, yellow-and-black hazard stripes on doors, roll-up garage doors, rooftop AC units and vents. The perimeter wall is grey concrete topped with barbed wire. The central courtyard is bright green grass with a boxing-style fight ring with red ropes in the middle and a glowing yellow spawn pad near the south side. Vibrant but grounded palette: greys, rust orange, muted teal, deep green, warm yellow accents.

No people, no vehicles moving, no text, no logos, no watermark.
