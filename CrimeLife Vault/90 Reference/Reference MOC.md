# Reference MOC

Places, pipelines, an index of every CrimeLife document outside the vault, and the archive of original prompts.

## Where things are
- [[Places and Studio Windows]]: FPS System and Robbery System, which file, what they share, the rule never to edit Robbery unless named, and the save rule (the developer saves; Claude never publishes).
- [[Four Places Survey 2026-11-14]]: measured contents of the four places to be merged into CrimeLife Tycoon, and the HQ blueprint's stages and money lines.
- [[Compound Floor Plan]]: the builder's drawing set for the tycoon base (361 × 300, rooms around a yard, G + F2 + roof terrace + bunker, 3D block model). Supersedes the HQ blueprint.
- [[Tycoon Market Research 2026-11]]: what holds players in Roblox tycoon games in 2025 to 2026, the crime competitors, and the policy limits.
- [[Docs Index]]: the repo's code mirrors, specs, plans, ledgers, the code graph, and the CrimeLife-relevant files at the top of `Documents/Roblox`.
- [[Original Prompts]]: every original prompt, one line each. What was asked for, against `70 Batches`, what happened.

## Pipelines in use
- [[Animation Pipeline]]: procedural viewmodel motion and its KeyframeSequence export (in use); the Blender rig-and-clip route (ready, not yet used).
- [[Figma and UI Art Pipeline]]: Figma slices at 4x, white icons, baked titles, the `Theme` / `UITheme` registries, the font substitutions.
- [[Icon Pipeline]]: weapon, tab and HUD icons from Figma or supplied PNGs, uploaded and wired to one owner each.

## Pipelines ready but not yet used in CrimeLife
- [[Blender Pipeline]]: headless Blender for clips, rigs and camera tracks.
- [[Asset Kit Pipeline]]: props and map dressing as plain Roblox Parts.
- [[GFX and Thumbnails]]: thumbnails, icons and badge art from Studio reference shots.
- [[Trailer and Cinematic Pipeline]]: Blender animates, the game renders; gameplay trailers from screen recordings.
