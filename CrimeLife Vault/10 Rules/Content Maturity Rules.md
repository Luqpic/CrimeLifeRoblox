# Content Maturity Rules

## Rule

- **CrimeLife is a crime shooter**: guns, melee, robbery, police and SWAT quest givers, enemy thugs. Keep it within Roblox's content rules for weapons and violence, and fill in the maturity questionnaire honestly before publishing (the developer's step).
- **The target maturity label is not recorded.** Ask the developer before adding anything that could change it: realistic blood or gore, alcohol, drugs, gambling-style paid random items, romance.
- **Ask before adding realistic blood decals, severed body parts or gore** (carried from OBT, where the developer kept blood non-realistic). The knife's "bleed" is damage over time (`BleedingUntil`); any visual for it stays non-realistic. What it looks like today is not recorded ([[FPS-Knife-Bleed-Melee]]).
- **Ask before copying real brands or a real agency's insignia** onto outfits, weapons or signs. The six quest givers wear the security, police and SWAT uniforms already on the police-side enemy templates ([[FPS-Quest-Givers-Law-Enforcement-Outfits]]); whether those carry real insignia is not recorded.
- **New free-model assets go through the review folder** and every script in them is read first ([[Safety and Security Rules]]).

## Why

- OBT (same developer) set a Moderate label target so the game reaches Roblox Select, and kept blood non-realistic. CrimeLife has no recorded target; this rule keeps the decision with the developer rather than guessing.
- Roblox restricts experiences with some content (alcohol and romance were noted on OBT as 18+ triggers in the questionnaire); a crime theme makes these easy to add by accident.

## How to apply

- Before publishing: the developer reviews the questionnaire. Record the chosen label in `80 Decisions` ([[Decisions MOC]]) once they pick one.
- Text rules: [[Player-Facing Text]].
