# Police and Wanted

**Skills:** `roblox-npc-ai`, `roblox-game-systems`, `roblox-physics` (load these for work here; add `roblox-ui` for the meter)

Crimelife City only, since 2026-11-17 ([[2026-11-17 Police and Wanted]]).

## What it does
Each player has a wanted level of 0 to 5 stars. Heist alarms, hurting police and crimes a witness sees raise it. Police exist only for wanted players: a squad sized by stars spawns out of sight, heads for the robbery or the player, and fights to kill. Armed Police join from 3 stars. Break every officer's (and guard's) sight and the stars drain one at a time; a death from real damage clears them and drops the haul, a character reset keeps them (the haul is still lost), and banking does not clear them. The Bank and NewsStation have stationed guards. A star meter shows the level. The six quest givers are now crooked cops ([[Quests and Dialogue]]).

## How it works (numbers from `Constants`, 2026-11-17)
- **Heat.** A number 0 to `MAX_HEAT` 5, replicated as player attributes `WantedHeat` and `WantedStars` (= floor). Not saved; leaving clears it.
- **Alarms** set heat to at least `ALARM_LEVELS`: Gas2Go 1, SlowFoods 1, NewsStation 2, Bank 3, for every participant at the trigger (the Bank: `bank:GetParticipants()` plus the trigger player, who may not be enrolled yet).
- **Police hits:** `POLICE_HIT_HEAT` 0.25 per hit, `POLICE_KILL_HEAT` 1 per kill (a killing hit gives both, 1.25). Hits on one officer by one player within `HIT_MERGE_WINDOW` 0.1 s count once (a shotgun blast or a swing is one hit). Bleed ticks fire no `Damaged` (measured: one Knife stab +0.25, five ticks +0). A dead or departed attacker's events are ignored.
- **Crimes in public:** a kill of any NPC +`PUBLIC_KILL_HEAT` 0.5, hurting a `Witness`-tagged NPC +`PUBLIC_HURT_HEAT` 0.25, only when a witness within `WITNESS_RANGE` 120 has line of sight to the attacker. The victim is never its own witness. A player who has left (`Parent` not `Players`) gets no heat and is dropped at the next step. Attacking a player gives nothing; NPC weapons and heist hazards give nothing.
- **Seen.** `WantedSeen` is true while any live, non-leaving officer, or any guard, within `SEEN_RANGE` 150 has line of sight (`Sight.inSight`: legs, root, head and ±`SIGHT_PROBE_SIDE` 1.5 across; parts with transparency >= `SEE_THROUGH_TRANSPARENCY` 0.3 do not block), or while the player is a participant of an active robbery and still in the building or within its `EscapeDistance` of the anchor.
- **Drain.** After any rise in heat a search window of `SEARCH_SECONDS` 20 must pass; then, unseen for `DRAIN_INTERVAL` 8 s, heat drops one step (to the integer below, or minus 1), and again every 8 s. Being seen restarts the 8 s. Checked at `SEEN_CHECK_HZ` 2. `WantedDraining` is true while the unseen timer runs. A death from real damage clears to 0; a self-reset keeps the heat (next bullet).
- **Reset versus damage death (2026-11-18, developer decision: "a character reset keeps the wanted stars").** The server keeps one record of damage per player, `hurtAt[player]` in `WantedService`, written by two server sources: a `HealthChanged` drop the player survives (`0 < new < old` and `new < MaxHealth`, so a MaxHealth clamp does not count), and a Blaster `Tagged` hit on the character while it has no ForceField (this covers a lethal one-shot, because `Tagged` fires a frame before `Died`). `markHurt(player)` writes it by hand (the Bank death pit calls it just before `Health = 0`). A drop to 0 is not counted, because a reset looks exactly like that on the server. `_onDied` (every character goes through `_watchCharacter`): damage within `RESET_DAMAGE_SECONDS` 5 clears the heat as before and forgets the record; otherwise, with heat above 0, the heat stays and the search window and the drain timer restart at the death, so a reset is never a faster way out than hiding. The squad stays with its player through the 6 s respawn (it used to dissolve). The haul is still forfeited by a death, a reset included.
- **Squads** (`SQUAD_BY_STARS`): 1 star 2 Police; 2 stars 4; 3 stars 4 + 2 Armed; 4 stars 4 + 4; 5 stars 4 + 6. Armed from `ARMED_FROM_STARS` 3. Server cap `POLICE_CAP` 24, shared highest stars first. Top-up every `TOPUP_INTERVAL` 5 s (and at once after an alarm); the dispatcher ticks every `DISPATCH_TICK` 1 s. When stars fall, officers above the new target leave by the leave path at the next tick, per kind, Armed Police first (`SquadPlan.surplus`; measured: 5 -> 2 stars, the 6 Armed left and were gone in 7.5 s, 4 Police stayed).
- **Spawn points.** After an alarm: a ring `ALARM_RING_MIN` 60 to `ALARM_RING_MAX` 120 studs around the location's `StatusBillboardAnchor`; otherwise, and as the fallback, `TOPUP_RING_MIN` 90 to `TOPUP_RING_MAX` 160 around the player. A point must be off the plots and robbery buildings, on road or footpath ground within `GROUND_PROBE` 5 below (stood `SPAWN_HEIGHT` 3 above), out of the player's sight, and reachable by path (`POLICE_AGENT`) to the player or, inside a building, to its `*ExitSpawn`. A search samples up to `SPAWN_SAMPLES` 2,000 points (yielding every 50); the cheap checks (boxes, ground, sight) are free, and the path checks share `SPAWN_PATH_BUDGET` 20 `ComputeAsync` calls per player per round. A round stops at the first search that finds nothing, and an alarm ring that missed is not searched again that round. Measured at the city spawn plaza: 4 in 3,000 samples pass, and 10 officers arrive within 5 s ([[2026-11-17 Police Slow Fill]]).
- **Behaviour.** Officers run the gang `EnemyAI` with `targetFilter` = `WantedStars >= 1` (refused mid-fight drops the target; no retaliation on an unwanted shooter) and `preferTarget` = their squad's player. `PoliceDispatcher.believedPosition` decides where they `investigate`: during an active robbery near its building the robbery entrance or the player's position (`WantedService.inActiveRobbery`); at every other time, the search window included, the last spot an officer or guard saw the player (`lastSeen`). There is no live tracking (developer decision, 2026-11-18). A squad that never saw the player gets no target and patrols around its `spawnPosition`. The search is the EnemyAI Search (walk to the spot, look around once, hold); officers re-engage only through their own sight. A squad whose player is cleared, dead or gone takes another wanted player it can see or leaves: refuse-all filter, patrol `LEAVE_DISTANCE` 200 away, despawn when no player sees it or after `LEAVE_TIMEOUT` 60 s.
- **Guards.** Bank 4 (only during a session, as before); NewsStation `NEWSSTATION_GUARDS` 3 always posted, respawn at their post 60 s after a death outside a session. Both shoot only that robbery's participants and players with 1+ star; `GuardDamage` 6 per shot every 0.9 s. Guards count as witnesses and for "seen". Guards are anchored at their posts and never move. NPC fire (police, gangs, guards) never damages a `Witness`-tagged model (`canDamageHumanoid`): a police volley through a guard leaves it untouched, so `GuardsCleared` needs player kills; players still damage guards and police.
- **Meter.** Five text-glyph stars (U+2605) on a `UITheme` plate, 148 x 36, top centre beside the heist column (right of it on desktop, left of it when the smaller viewport side is under 500); each star clipped by its fraction over the glyph's ink; red pulsing stroke while seen, the next star blinks while draining; hidden at 0 and while a `UI.Panels` panel is open.
- **Kills** of police pay as any enemy (XP, 30 % cash drop) and count on the saved `PoliceKills` (by design, through the existing enemy reward path; not measured in this batch).

Measured end to end on 2026-11-17 (`CityMerge/police/final-run.md`): SlowFoods 1 star, 2 police; banked at 201 studs while seen, cash once, the star stayed; out of sight behind a rise, 0 at 8.0 s and the police gone at the same sample; died while wanted at Gas2Go, 2,000 forfeited, heat 0; Bank 3 stars, 4 + 2; NewsStation 2 stars, guards 6 hp a second; 10 police held for 125 s, never more.

## Admin hooks (2026-11-20)
`WantedService.setStars(player, n)` is `clear` then `addHeat` (fires `Changed` twice; the dispatcher reads the attribute at its tick, so no squad breaks up). `PoliceDispatcher.spawnOfficer` accepts no owner (no squad) and returns the character; `spawnLoose(template, position)` makes loose officers for the admin's SPAWN POLICE (patrol, engage only wanted players, stay until killed or cleared; Armed Police from the third). Measured: 3 spawned gave 2 Police and 1 Armed Police tagged `AdminSpawned`. Loose admin police count against `POLICE_CAP` in `countLive()` until cleared, so a real wanted player can get fewer squad officers near the cap. See [[Admin Panel]].

## Heist police and the squad hold (2026-11-20)
Details: [[2026-11-20 Heist Police and Bank Guards]], [[2026-11-20 Readable Gunfights and Level Toughness]].
- **Heist police.** `ServerScriptService.Heist.Services.HeistPolice` is a component on the Bank and NewsStation sessions. From the session's start, `Config.HeistPolice` sends Police inside the building: Bank 3 at 120 s then +2 every 90 s (at most 8 alive); NewsStation 2 at 120 s then +1 every 90 s (at most 4). The Bank's `Config.HeistPoliceVault` sends Armed Police when the vault door opens: 4 at once, +2 every 60 s, at most 8. They are made by `PoliceDispatcher.spawnLoose(template, position, targetFilter?)` (it returns the character and its EnemyAI) at `HeistPoliceSpawn1..3` markers, run the gang EnemyAI, and target that robbery's participants and wanted players inside the building, never bystanders. The seal removes them; squad officers stay for the getaway.
- **Sharing `POLICE_CAP` 24.** `PoliceDispatcher.room()` is 24 minus every live police AI. A wave fills only free room (re-checked before each officer) and waits when there is none; it never removes a squad officer, and squads count heist officers as taken room.
- **Squad hold.** `PoliceDispatcher.addSquadHold` and `isHeld`: a held player asks for 0 officers in the top-up (live ones still count) and keeps the alarm ring for the first round after. HeistPolice holds the squad of the robbery's participants and the players inside when it started, until the first wave (120 s); it also registers with `WantedService.addActiveRobbery` so a held robber counts as seen and the stars hold. The stars and the HUD still rise at the alarm.
- **Guards.** The Bank's four guards are Security and Police rigs, NewsStation's three are Police rigs; they now fire real shots with the shooter-token rhythm (see [[Enemies and Spawner]]). `Constants`: `HEIST_POLICE_TICK` 1, `HEIST_POLICE_SCATTER` 1.5.
- **Known limits.** (Until the next morning the vault Armed Police spawned at the B-arrival markers; they now stay in the lobby.) squads cannot spawn while every robber is in the vault (the entrance lookup covers `Workspace.Bank`, not `BankVault`); a non-participant left in the lobby after the hold is unseen and drains. See [[Open Items]].
- **2026-11-20 morning ([[2026-11-20 Walking Bank Guards and Edit Rigs]]).** Bank waves alternate two sides (before and after the obby pit); the armed plan stays in the lobby (`vaultMarkers`, `vaultFilter` Lobby zone, `patrolRadius` 12, not steered); steering checks one side a tick. `HeistPolice.markersIn(folder, prefix)` takes each marker's Edit rig. EnemyAI reads `ai.patrolRadius` before `Constants.PATROL_RADIUS`.


## Crouch stealth (2026-11-20)
`isSeen` uses the player's `StealthRange` instead of `SEEN_RANGE` when it is shorter (officers and guards), so a hidden wanted player 25+ studs away is unseen and the stars drain. [[2026-11-20 Crouch Stealth]]

## Extend it
- Tune by number in `Constants` only (heat amounts, drain, squad table, rings, cap). Guard damage and rate are per location Config (`HeistShared.Config`, `NewsStationShared.Config`).
- A new robbery location: add its name to `ALARM_LEVELS`, make it call `WantedService.onAlarm` where it announces `NotifyRobberyTriggered`, register its active-robbery check with `addActiveRobbery` through `Getaway:IsNear`, and add its building and zones to the dispatcher's robbery boxes.
- A new witness: tag the template `Witness` (guards also `WitnessRole = "Guard"`).
- A new police type: a template in `ServerStorage.EnemyTemplates`, a key in `POLICE_TEMPLATES`, a column in `SQUAD_BY_STARS`.

## Watch out
- **An `execute_luau` `require` gets its own module copy.** Heat set through it shows on the meter (same attributes) but never drains or dispatches. Drive heat through the game's paths or a temporary server Script ([[Luau and Scripting Traps]]).
- Police wait at the SlowFoods door: its interior is off the navmesh. They engage when the robber comes out.
- Police no longer follow the player's live position in the search window (changed 2026-11-18). Measured: a robber teleported about 275 studs away behind a rise was never seen again in 20 s (before: seen again from t12); the four officers stood at 3 to 5 studs from the last-seen spot.
- Officers walk to the last spot they saw you and hold there until they see you again or a star drops (the stars drain after the window, so squads shrink: measured 5 stars, 10 officers, then 8 at 48 s and 6 at 56 s, cleared at 60 s). Break sight behind a building or a rise, then move away.
- At NewsStation the full 2-star squad took about 32 s (3 of 4 at 18 s): spawn points around it are scarce.
- The cap, squads sharing it and every multi-player rule are proven by tests only (Solo has one player).
- Bank participants who join after the trigger get no alarm heat.
- A lethal blow from full health with no `Tagged` (a guard or laser hit on a robber not hurt in the last 5 s) reads as a self-reset, so the stars carry. That is the safe direction (no exploit can clear stars). The Bank death pit is covered by `markHurt`: measured heat 1 to 0 in the pit under a ForceField, with no run of the old code before it.
- There is no fall damage in the city and an RPG blast never hurts its own shooter (`explode` skips the shooter), so those two cannot count as damage today; a future server fall damage will count through `HealthChanged`.
- Police shots on a ForceField-protected robber fire `Tagged` but deal nothing, so `Tagged` under a ForceField is ignored (the first Task 1 run cleared heat 4 to 0 on a reset because of it).
- An idle Armed Police on flat ground may not fire until it moves: standing on the plaza, the Scar L muzzle sits about 1.5 studs under the floor (y -0.36), so the muzzle line-of-sight check fails (0 shots in the static two-in-a-line test; 150 shots in the real chase).
- Not seen by eye or on two players: the search behaviour with a second player, the sidestep ([[Enemies and Spawner]]), and the phone.
- The 120 ms server step while a round of 10 officers builds did not reproduce (2026-11-18): builds already run one per frame (a path check that yields precedes each), at most 1 per frame across 15 builds of 4.7 to 22.3 ms; the worst step in the 10-officer round was 43.9 ms against a 45.1 ms no-police baseline. No change was made.

## Patrols (2026-11-20)
`ServerScriptService.Wanted.PolicePatrols` (WantedRunner starts it; `Constants` `PATROL_*`) keeps 6 loose Police (attribute `Patrol`) on the roads: each stands up 120 to 300 studs from a random player on road or footpath ground, out of every player's sight and with a path to them (`_findSpawn` with that player and a 4-path budget), and walks legs to road points 60 to 200 studs away (a new leg on arrival or after 60 s). A dead patrol is replaced 30 s later; one 600+ studs from every player and unseen is recycled near a player at once. They engage wanted players and criminal NPCs on sight (faction fights, [[Enemies and Spawner]]); a wanted player believed (`PoliceDispatcher.believedPosition`) within 250 studs draws them in. They count against `POLICE_CAP`. [[2026-11-20 Smooth Enemies Patrols and Dropped Duffels]]
