# Weapons and Blaster

**Skills:** `roblox-game-systems`, `roblox-networking`, `roblox-security` (load these for work here)

## What it does
Every gun, melee weapon and the RPG runs on one shared, data-driven blaster: stats are attributes on the Tool, one client controller and one server resolver drive every weapon. The server is the authority for hits, damage, headshots, knockback, stun, bleed and explosions.

## Where it lives
- Weapon Tools: `ServerStorage.Weapons.*` (moved out of `StarterPack`; granted by the shop, see [[Weapon Shop and Upgrades]]). Enemy copies live under `ServerStorage.EnemyTemplates.<Type>.<Weapon>`.
- `ReplicatedStorage.Blaster.Constants` (attribute names), `Blaster.Scripts.BlasterController`, `Blaster.Scripts.ViewModelController`, `Blaster.Scripts.WeaponViewmodelMotion` (see [[Viewmodels and Weapon Animation]]).
- `ReplicatedStorage.Blaster.Utility.castRays`, `.drawRayResults`, `.findOwningHumanoid`, `.canDamageHumanoid`; `Blaster.Effects` (`impactEffect`, `laserBeamEffect`, `projectileEffect`); `Blaster.Objects` (`MeleeImpact`, `RPGRocket`); `Blaster.ViewModels.*`; `Blaster.Remotes` (`DamageDirection`, `KillConfirmed`).
- Server: `ServerScriptService.Blaster.Scripts.ShotResolver`, `.DamageDirectionService` ([[Damage Direction]]).
- Repo copies: `CrimeLifeRoblox/FPSSystem/Blaster/ShotResolver.luau`, `FPSSystem/Blaster/BuildNewPistolsShotguns.luau` (re-runnable build for the eight 2026-09-24 guns and the RPG).

## How it works
- **Attributes, not forks.** Per-weapon behaviour is opt-in data: `damage`, `rateOfFire`, `magazineSize`, `range`, spread, `raysPerShot`, `rayRadius`, `reloadTime`, fire mode (semi / auto / burst), `infiniteAmmo`, `knockbackForce`, `knockbackOnKill`, `swingDelay`, `meleeHitEffect`, `stunDuration`, bleed (`BLEED_DURATION_ATTRIBUTE` / `BLEED_DAMAGE_ATTRIBUTE`), `backstabMultiplier`, `Category`, `Price`, `Gamepass`, `ShopViewRotation`, `viewModel`, and the RPG's `explosionRadius` / `explosionEdgeDamage` / `explosionKnockback` / `explosionFlingSpeed` / `projectile`. Every unset attribute leaves the weapon on the original path.
- **Shots.** The client casts, the server re-validates every shot in `ShotResolver`. A hit resolves to its owner with `findOwningHumanoid` (walks up through a carried Tool), so a carried weapon no longer absorbs hits. Headshot ×2 on `Head`; a weapon with `backstabMultiplier` uses that rule instead.
- **Kills and rewards.** Damage goes through `TakeDamage` + `Tagged` / `Eliminated`; cash, XP and quest credit all listen to `Eliminated` (see [[Economy and Cash]], [[Leveling and Renown]], [[Quests and Dialogue]]). `KillConfirmed` tells the killer the victim's name for the kill toast ([[Notifications]]).
- **Knockback.** Per-ray push `knockbackForce / raysPerShot`; with `knockbackOnKill` (all shotguns, `knockbackForce` 38) the full force goes on the killing ray only, through `flingRagdoll` ([[Ragdoll]]). Melee keeps the per-ray push on every swing.
- **Tracers.** A miss is drawn only `MISS_TRACER_LENGTH` = 150 studs (0.75 s); hits draw to the impact point. `LASER_BEAM_VISUAL_SPEED` is 200 studs/s.
- **Burst fire** is client-side (server validates each shot). The shotgun reloads shell by shell and can be cancelled by firing.
- **Muzzle.** Every weapon must carry `MuzzleAttachment` in both its Tool and viewmodel; melee carries an empty one. It sets flash, tracer start, sound origin and shop preview orientation, not aim (in third person not even the shot origin, since 2026-11-18, below).
- **Third-person shots (2026-11-18).** In third person (the toggle-view key) `BlasterController.shoot` casts from the head toward the point the crosshair hits, through the new pure module `ReplicatedStorage.Blaster.Utility.thirdPersonAim(camera, head, range, cast)`. The crosshair ray runs along the camera's look starting level with the head (projected onto the look line, so nothing between the shoulder camera, 5.79 studs behind, and the character counts), through the existing `castRays` (radius `CROSSHAIR_RAY_RADIUS` 0.05). With no hit within range the aim point is `CROSSHAIR_FAR_DISTANCE` 1000 studs along the camera look, so only the direction counts (a melee miss was 25.92 degrees off the camera look before, 0.30 after). A hit level with the head keeps the camera's direction; range 0 or nil casts nothing; `CFrame.lookAt` takes the camera's up vector so a straight-up look stays defined. The tracer still starts at the muzzle and the flash stays on the muzzle's `FlashEmitter`. First person is unchanged and `validateShot`'s origin check is unchanged (`POSITION_BUFFER_CONSTANT` 5). Why: [[Camera and Input Traps]].
- **RPG** (Category `Launcher`, `Gamepass = "RPG"`, no `Price`): its ray only finds the landing point; `ShotResolver.explode` fires after `distance / PROJECTILE_SPEED` (250), 200 damage at centre to 60 at the 16-stud rim, never the shooter. The blast burst numbers live in `Blaster.Constants` since 2026-11-18: `RPG_BLAST_RATE` 60, `RPG_BLAST_EMIT_SECONDS` 0.25, `RPG_BLAST_LIFETIME` 2 (same values as before; read back live: emitter Rate 60 at 0.1 s, Enabled true at 0.15 s and false by 0.40 s, blast removed after 2.02 s). Engine `Explosion` is flash only (BlastPressure 0). The killed are flung, survivors get `RagdollController.stagger` 1.2 s plus a push. `HideWhenEmpty` parts (the loaded rocket) follow `_ammo`. Pass id is `Monetization.Constants` RPG `gamepassId` ([[Gamepasses]]).
- **APS is a machine pistol (developer decision, 2026-11-18).** Category Pistol (was AutoRifle), Auto, 750 rpm kept (the real cyclic rate), magazine 20 (26), damage 18 (14), range 700 (750), spread 2.2 (3.5), reload 1.8 kept, recoil and impulse as the Glock 18, Price 650 (600), `Description` "Full-auto machine pistol, 20 rounds, steadier fire". Against the Glock 18 (1000 rpm, mag 33, Price 850): 225 versus 300 damage per second, 360 versus 594 per magazine. Kill-reward tier keys on fire mode, so the APS stays in the AutoRifle tier for rewards, like the Glock 18. The mesh is still the rifle-sized underwater-rifle mesh (7.02 x 2.26 x 0.58 studs against 1.57 to 2.72 for the other pistols), with the rifle hold clips; the developer decides on a mesh.
- **Roster** (as recorded): starters plus loadout batches 2026-09-11 (M1911, Mateba 2006M, AKM, HCAR, Kriss Vector, Scar L, six more unnamed in the log), 2026-09-15/16 (M14 EBR, AR-18, HK G3, APS, PB, MP443, Glock 17, S&W 29, Itacha Mag-10, Benelli M4, Spas 12), 2026-09-24 (Glock 18, SIG M17, M45A1, USP Compact, Remington 870, Sawed-Off 870, MP-153, SPAS-13, RPG). Melee: Crowbar, Baton, Knife ([[Melee]]). One log counts 34 guns in its sweep (2026-09-24).

- In the city, `ServerScriptService.Blaster.Scripts.Blaster.validateFireInterval` (pure, `(record?, timestamp, rateOfFire, receivedAt)`, returns the new record or nil when refused) is called last by `validateShot`, which stores the record per player and weapon name and passes its own `GetServerTimeNow()`. Since 2026-11-18 a shot must keep pace on **two clocks**, each with `INTERVAL_TOLERANCE` 0.9 (both numbers in `Blaster.Constants`): (1) the claimed timestamp, at least 0.9 x 60 / rateOfFire after the last accepted one, as before; (2) the server receive time: each accepted shot pushes `due` on by 0.9 x interval from max(due, receivedAt), and a shot that arrives more than `ARRIVAL_SLACK` 0.25 s before `due` is refused. Idle time therefore banks at most 0.25 s: an idle burst of 12 back-dated shots at once now passes 3 at 600 rpm and 6 on the MP9 (1200 rpm), 1 + floor(0.25 / (0.9 x interval)), where all 12 passed before (also at 750, 1000 and 1200 rpm; 90 rpm 2, 30 rpm 1). Honest fire with +-30 ms jitter loses nothing at 600, 1200 and 90 rpm (0 refused of 100); an honest 600 rpm stream through a 250 ms stall loses 0 shots, through a 600 ms stall 4 (pinned as the accepted loss). Live: AK47 held 1.25 s after 3 s idle, 11 sent, 11 accepted; APS full auto 12 of 12. A nil or non-number rate-of-fire attribute is accepted. `validateShootArguments` refuses non-canonical hit keys. Tests `ShotRate_Test` (13 cases, pinning the 0.85-of-an-interval refusal, the stall cases and the idle burst; they go red if the arrival clock is removed), `ThirdPersonAim_Test` (8 cases), `BlasterGuard_Test` ([[2026-11-15 FPS Hardening In City]]).

## Third person over the shoulder (2026-11-20)
Built on the developer's brief "Add a third person POV for the FPS system (can toggle); reuse OBT's weaponry system". Decision: OBT's shoulder camera ported into CrimeLife's blaster, not OBT's gun engine ([[Admin, Animation and Camera Decisions]]).
- **Modules:** `ReplicatedStorage.Blaster.Scripts.ShoulderCamera` (render step `Camera-1`), `Blaster.Utility.shoulderMath` (pure: `clampSide`, `pitchOf`, `acceptPitch`, `due`, `smooth`), `ViewModelController` (third person is now the shoulder camera; the old fixed-offset camera and its root turn are gone), `BlasterController` (keys), `TouchInputController` (VIEW and SWAP buttons, SWAP only in third person), `Blaster.Constants` (`SHOULDER_*`, `AIM_PITCH_*`, swap and pad keys). Server `ServerScriptService.Blaster.Scripts.AimPitch` writes the character attribute `AimPitch`; client `AimPitchView` eases other players' waist and neck to it. Test: `ShoulderMath_Test` (5).
- **Behaviour:** a gun that comes out starts in third person; **V** switches to first person and the choice is remembered across equips and respawns (module `preferThird`, player attribute `ViewMode`). **Z** or pad R3 swaps the shoulder (the equipped Tool's controller only), DPadUp toggles the view on a pad. A wall clamp pulls the offset in. Aim (RMB) is the shoulder zoom: tighter offset, FOV 50, sensitivity 0.5. Measured: equip offset (1.75, 1.40), zoom 9; wall 1.7 studs right gives offset 1.20; aim gives (1.10, 1.30); crouch plus aim Y 0.30; looking 45 up gives neck 18 degrees and server `AimPitch` 0.79; a gun at a dummy 50 studs ahead, right shoulder 6 rounds 90 damage, left shoulder 6 rounds 80. No gun here has a scope, so "scoped guns go to first-person sights" has nothing to apply to (no bob or shake in the port: `Bobbing Camera` and `CameraRecoiler` own those).
- **Facing:** while the shoulder camera runs it is the only writer of `AutoRotate` and the root yaw (`SmoothShiftLock` returns early); a roll or ledge climb (`MoveAction`) wins the facing, and `exit()` turns `AutoRotate` on only with no `MoveAction`. Strafe 2.5 s: facing within 0.1 degrees of the camera.
- **Panels and states:** a panel, dialogue, seat, PlatformStand or death releases the shoulder view and it returns after; a held aim comes back (module `wantAiming`).
- **Shots** still start at the head (2026-11-18 `thirdPersonAim`); the camera moved, the rule did not. Same tracer and flash from the muzzle.
- **Fixed bug:** every Tool's `BlasterController` heard Z, so two tools swapped twice and cancelled out; only the equipped controller now acts ([[Camera and Input Traps]]). The touch Reload button had no callback (a tap did nothing); it has one now.
- Camera layering and the "Shoulder" source: [[Camera Authority]]. Movement and rolls: [[Character Movement and Animation]]. The admin spectate unequips the tool first so the shoulder lets go ([[Admin Panel]]).

## NPC shots through ShotResolver (2026-11-20)
Details: [[2026-11-20 Readable Gunfights and Level Toughness]].
- `ShotResolver.applyDamage`: when the shooter is an NPC, the hit goes through `Combat.npcDamage` (range falloff x1 to 25 studs, x0.5 at 90 and beyond, then the victim player's level armor). Player shots are unchanged.
- Guards (`Heist.Components.GuardPost`) now fire through `ShotResolver.resolveShot` from their weapon's muzzle with no `claimedTags`, so the server raycast decides, and every client sees the tracer, flash and sound. Their weapon's damage is `GuardDamage` (6), set at spawn; the magazine is topped up before each shot.
- A hit on an NPC shooter flinches it (once a second at most). The fast-semi recoil trap that this batch found is in [[Physics and Character Traps]].

## Hit registration (2026-11-20)
- `validateTag` accepts a claimed hit when the ray passes within 10 studs of the target's pivot measured sideways, the pivot at most 3 studs behind the ray's start (was an angle that refused close hits while moving). [[2026-11-20 Hit Registration]]
- Player accessories and tools are `RayExclude` (`AccessoryFiltering`, template rule): incoming bullets pass through hair, hats and coats.

## Extend it
- New gun: run the donor-skeleton build (`BuildNewPistolsShotguns.luau`): same-class donor, keep its `Body` as a hidden anchor, hang the new mesh off it, never replace the core part. Add a `profiles` entry in `WeaponViewmodelMotion` or first person never starts. Give it `Category` (+ `Price` or `Gamepass`) and the shop picks it up. Upload a `TextureId` icon.
- New behaviour: add an opt-in attribute to `Blaster.Constants` and read it in the shared code; never branch per weapon.

## Watch out
- A missing `MuzzleAttachment` aborts the weapon's setup before equip/fire/reload connect; the Tool then behaves as a plain held object.
- A missing `profiles` entry asserts in `WeaponViewmodelMotion.new` and kills `BlasterController.new` (no first person).
- Copying a weapon does not redirect welds that point outside the copied subtree: rebuild viewmodel joints against the viewmodel's own `Body`.
- Reference rigs disagree where the left hand hangs (body vs magazine); assume nothing.
- `raysPerShot` is only listed explicitly on shotguns; a clone can inherit nothing.
- The Glock 17, Benelli M4 and Spas 12 Tool `MuzzleAttachment` sits at the barrel end (measured to 0.000 of the barrel-end point, 2026-11-17) and carries a `FlashEmitter`. Since 2026-11-18 the other 12 guns (APS, AR-18, Glock 18, HK G3, Itacha Mag-10, M14 EBR, M45A1, MP443, PB, S&W 29, SIG M17, USP Compact) have a barrel-end muzzle (Orientation 0, -90, 0) and a same-class `FlashEmitter` (Glock 17's or the Mateba 2006M's; all 26 existing emitters are identical: texture 14364353606, Rate 2, life 0.125, size to 1.25). The barrel end came from a raycast front-face map; the method matched the 2026-11-17 capture points (Benelli 0.49 versus 0.48, Spas 12 0.29 versus 0.28, Glock 17 0.43 versus 0.47); MP443's height is estimated from the Glock 17 (+-0.05). The flashes were not seen by eye. The 5 long guns (APS, AR-18, HK G3, M14 EBR, Itacha Mag-10) could only be moved once the third-person origin no longer came from the muzzle.
- The RPG's blast is no longer an engine `Explosion` (its bang cannot be silenced): `ShotResolver.explode` clones `ReplicatedStorage.Blaster.Objects.RPGBlast`, a particle burst. Damage, `Tagged` / `Damaged` / `Eliminated` are unchanged. The burst is smaller than the old fireball and was not judged by eye.
- Mossberg 590 and Spas 12 have no `Pump` part or `PumpJoint`: their fore-ends are fused into long meshes, so they cannot rack. The fix is a mesh split in Blender plus an upload (the developer); `WeaponViewmodelMotion` already moves any `Pump` joint.
- Every Tool has a `Description` (38 of 38, longest 53 characters); `WeaponShopService.CATALOG_ATTRIBUTES` carries it to the shop's detail card.
- A shotgun's ammo readout counts shells up through the shell-by-shell reload (`GuiController.updateAmmoText`); magazine weapons keep the dashes while reloading.
- Dry-fire cue: a press that the client's own fire-rate cooldown holds back plays `GuiController.DryFire` for Semi fire only (`startShooting`; never for Auto, Burst or melee), once per press. Burst re-press fixed 2026-11-18: M16A1 re-pressed 0.08 s into a burst gave 0 cues, the Glock 17 Semi control 1. The server never answers a refused shot, so the cue cannot play for one.
- **No ammo resync after a refused shot.** The server writes the Tool's replicated `_ammo` only when a shot is accepted or a reload finishes, and no remote answers a refusal, so a refused shot leaves the client one round lower (it reloads one round early; the client never shows more rounds than the server has; the next equip resyncs). Nothing was built for it. This is why `ARRIVAL_SLACK` is 0.25 s: a hitch of about 250 ms on a phone must cost no round.
- Studio cannot load the Tools' hold clips (6170771161 and 6170792878), so a hold pose measured in Studio is the default tool hold ([[Sound and Asset Traps]]); a published place may hold guns differently, not measured.
- Parked: no spec pins `validateShot`'s wiring of the receive clock, the controller's press stamp or its `thirdPersonAim` call (Play read-backs only). A target the shoulder camera sees but the head does not is hit on the wall in front of the head, by design, not measured. The revolvers' left-hand drift is not real (Mateba 2006M, S&W 29, Glock 17, Deagle, M1911 all 0.000 after a shot from a settled equip; a shot during the draw blend reads 0.56 to 0.68, the same for every weapon).
- Seen in passing: 1,645 `SetPrimaryPartCFrame` warnings per heist suite run, source not traced (Task 3 report).
- The repo copy `FPSSystem/Blaster/ShotResolver.luau` was last committed with per-weapon Spraypaint and has no `explode()`; Studio holds the newer code.
- Never reuse the old prop-impulse formula on characters (range-scaled; once flung a corpse at 584 studs/s).
- The `StarterCharacterScripts.Lean` line 25 error (direct `HumanoidRootPart` index) shows up in weapon testing; unfixed as of 2026-09-24.
- Third person (2026-11-20), open Minors from the review: the `AimPitch` server rate limit lets a 0 through at any rate, and the server cannot know the shoulder camera is on (cosmetic only); `ShoulderCamera.enter` calls `lockBuiltIn` unprotected, so a throw would leave the state set; crouching in third person snaps the camera instead of easing; the `ViewMode` attribute is not cleared on unequip; a per-frame cost the review wants trimmed (one raycast and a filter re-assign each frame). No pure test covers the remembered-mode logic. [[Open Items]]
