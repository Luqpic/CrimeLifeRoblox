# Client load-order audit (city place)

Measured 2026-11-15 in `Crimelife city.rbxl` (Edit). FPS place only read. Nothing saved or published.

## The defect class

A client script indexes with `.` an instance that reaches the client separately from the script itself, so on a slow join it may not exist yet when the line runs. Seen twice in the city:
- `StarterCharacterScripts.Lean` line 25: the character's `HumanoidRootPart` arrives about 39 ms after `Lean` (measured from `CharacterAdded`: Lean 0.032 s, HumanoidRootPart 0.071 s). Fixed in leg 2.
- `StarterPlayerScripts.WeaponaryShopController` line 20: sibling `SkinsCardController` not yet copied into PlayerScripts (2 of 3 leg-2 Plays); the shop and skins card were never built. Same Plays logged `Infinite yield possible on PlayerGui:WaitForChild("Custom Inventory")` from 7 scripts: the StarterGui copy also arrived more than 5 s late.

## Search

Every LuaSourceContainer under `StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts`, `StarterPlayer.StarterCharacterScripts`, `StarterGui` and `ReplicatedStorage` (the client-required modules live there; server-only modules there were included rather than guessed out), comments stripped, for these patterns:
- `script.Parent.<Name>` (sibling or ancestor path),
- `PlayerGui.<Name>` / `playerGui.<Name>`,
- `Character.<Name>` / `character.<Name>` / `char.<Name>`,
- `ReplicatedStorage.<Name>`,
- also `PlayerScripts.<Name>`, `Backpack.<Name>`, `LocalPlayer.<Child>` (0 hits),
- and `playerGui:FindFirstChild(` followed by unguarded use (1 hit, `ReplicatedStorage.UI.Panels:61`, guarded with `if gui and ...`: SAFE, not a row).

135 matching lines, plus the 3 Lean lines fixed in leg 2 (no longer matched): **138 rows, 4 FIX (3 done in leg 2, 1 in this leg), 134 SAFE.**

The SAFE verdict on ReplicatedStorage reads is observed, not guaranteed by anything this audit could test. It rests on: 0 such errors in 10 city Plays; in both failing Plays, `WeaponaryShopController` lines 12-19 (8 direct ReplicatedStorage paths) succeeded immediately before line 20 failed on a PlayerScripts sibling. If a ReplicatedStorage miss is ever seen, the same `WaitForChild` change applies to those rows.
